# Slideshow Inset Psychology

Understanding spatial peeking, peripheral anticipation, and focal framing in inset carousels.

## Cognitive ergonomics

1. **Peripheral Affordance (Peeking Next Card):**
   - Showing partial slices of adjacent slides on either side solves the classic "hidden carousel slide" problem without requiring intrusive banners. Users immediately perceive the horizontal catalog depth.
2. **Contained Framing (Card vs Full Bleed):**
   - Rounding the inset card creates a focused visual vignette, isolating the campaign message from the edges of the screen and reducing visual distraction.
3. **Pill State Morphing (`• • ▬ •`):**
   - Morphing the active pagination indicator from a circle into an elongated pill signals precise location and progress within the carousel sequence at a glance.
