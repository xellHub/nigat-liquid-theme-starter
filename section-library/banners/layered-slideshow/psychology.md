# Layered Slideshow Psychology

Understanding how multi-layer visual depth enhances product desirability and comprehension.

## Cognitive anchors

1. **Depth Perception Implies High Craftsmanship:**
   Flat, single-layer banners often blend into the web background as generic advertisements. Stacking distinct visual planes—an atmospheric environment, an elevated product subject, and a tactile detail crop—activates human depth perception and conveys premium craftsmanship.

2. **Hierarchical Attention Flow:**
   - **Plane 1 (Atmosphere):** Sets emotional tone in under 50ms.
   - **Plane 2 (Focal Hero):** Delivers immediate recognition of the product silhouette.
   - **Plane 3 (Accent Detail):** Validates quality with close-up texture or material proof.
   - **Plane 4 (Editorial Copy):** Connects the visual feeling to a concrete next step.

3. **Curated Momentum:**
   Slideshow pagination invites discovery without aggressive motion. Respecting user interaction (pausing on hover, providing explicit arrow and keyboard triggers) builds user comfort and agency.
