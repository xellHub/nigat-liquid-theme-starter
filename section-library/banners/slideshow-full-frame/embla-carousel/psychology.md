# Embla Carousel Psychology

Understanding visual immersion, tactile momentum, and cognitive pacing in full-frame carousels.

## Cognitive Ergonomics

1. **Gestural Agency & Tactile Feedback:**
   - Enabling natural swipe and drag physics creates an immediate sense of direct manipulation and control, significantly lowering friction compared to static button clicks alone.
2. **Backdrop Legibility & Cognitive Ease:**
   - High-contrast backdrops (`glass`, `card`, `fade`) resolve the classic hero banner dilemma: maintaining brand imagery vibrancy without compromising text legibility or forcing extreme dark overlays.
3. **Paced Progression & Temporal Orientation:**
   - Numerical counters (`01 / 03`) and line progress indicators give shoppers transparent spatial awareness of carousel length, preventing fatigue and encouraging exploration of subsequent slides.
