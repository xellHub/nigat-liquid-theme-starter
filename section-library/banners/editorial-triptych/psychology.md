# Editorial Triptych Psychology

Understanding visual balance, triadic harmony, and customer decision psychology within the 3-panel editorial banner.

## Cognitive ergonomics

1. **The Rule of Thirds & Triadic Harmony:**
   - The human brain processes 3-part groupings more readily than even numbers or sprawling matrices. A triptych feels complete, stable, and curated rather than endless.
2. **Side-by-Side Category Discovery:**
   - Customers often enter a store unsure whether they are looking for body care, facial treatments, or daily serums. Presenting all three with equal stature prevents premature drop-off and enables rapid self-segmentation.
3. **Subtle Micro-Interactions (Hover Scale):**
   - Individual hover zooms create tactile feedback, confirming that each panel is an independent, clickable portal without overwhelming the page with complex animations.
4. **Frosted Glass Affordances:**
   - Translucent glassmorphism pill buttons preserve the photographic integrity behind them while remaining distinctly recognizable as clickable navigation targets.
