# Editorial Triptych

A 3-panel editorial banner family presenting parallel brand pillars, category gateways, or campaign highlights side-by-side.

## Purpose

Unify three complementary product lines, campaign stories, or flagship collections into an edge-to-edge triptych with subtle vertical dividers, dual-font typography, and frosted glassmorphism action buttons.

## Physical composition

- **Triptych Grid:** 3 equal-width vertical columns with hairline dividers (`1px` translucent or white).
- **Background Imagery:** Full-bleed portrait photography with smooth scale-up on hover.
- **Overlay & Typography:** Dark gradient vignette with two-line typographic hierarchy (modern sans-serif followed by italic serif accent).
- **Glassmorphism CTAs:** Frosted translucent pill buttons with border glow and backdrop blur.
