# Editorial Carousel Hero Psychology

Guidance for presenting lifestyle-led product environments without cognitive friction or misleading claims.

## Attention sequence

1. **Ambient Spatial Context**: The full-bleed architectural interior immediately conveys living space scale, natural daylight warmth, and lifestyle aspiration.
2. **Editorial Hook**: The bold typography ("Made for the moments between") frames the furniture around human experience and comfort rather than raw commercial transactions.
3. **Focal Material Verification**: Subtle vector callouts ("Full-grain leather", "Cast steel base") invite visual inspection directly against the photography.
4. **Interactive Exploration Rail**: The bottom thumbnail rail suggests catalog breadth without visual clutter, encouraging active engagement and discovery.

## Ethical safeguards

- Ensure lifestyle imagery accurately represents actual product scale, texture, and finishes.
- Keep material annotations strictly factual (e.g. leather grade, metal type).
- Maintain accessible contrast over full-bleed photography via directional gradient masking.
