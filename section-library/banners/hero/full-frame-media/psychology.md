# Full Frame Media Hero Psychology

Understanding sensory immersion, dual-action clarity, and cognitive hierarchy in full-bleed hero banners.

## Cognitive ergonomics

1. **Sensory Immersion & Brand Framing:**
   - Cinematic background video and photographic depth trigger immediate emotional resonance, positioning the brand's aesthetic before cognitive evaluation begins.
2. **Dual Action Choice Hierarchy:**
   - Offering two calibrated actions (e.g. solid primary "Shop now" alongside outlined secondary "Explore collection") splits friction for disparate visitor intents—converting direct shoppers without alienating discovery-oriented visitors.
3. **Scrim Contrast & Legibility:**
   - Calibrated background overlay opacity preserves effortless reading contrast over dynamic video or rich photography, preventing cognitive fatigue.
