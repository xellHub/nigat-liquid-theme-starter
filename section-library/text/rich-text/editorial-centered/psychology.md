# Rich Text — Conversion Psychology

## Attention Flow

1. **Heading**: Catches scanning visitors with high-contrast headline summarizing brand ethos or promotion.
2. **Body Copy**: Provides succinct validation, humanizing the brand and establishing authentic voice.
3. **CTA**: Clear next step driving visitors to collection, about page, or story without high-friction pressure.

## Trust & Credibility

- Uncluttered presentation reinforces premium positioning.
- Generous whitespace around narrative content gives visitors time to digest brand claims.
- Restrained, legible type supports effortless reading.
