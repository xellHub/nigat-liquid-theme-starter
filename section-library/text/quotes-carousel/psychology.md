# Quotes Carousel Psychology

## Cognitive Impact & Design Mechanics

1. **Authority Transference & Halo Effect**:
   - Featuring recognized institutional or publication marks (e.g., Vogue, Allure, Architectural Digest) above the quote triggers immediate authority transference. Shoppers unconsciously map the established prestige and editorial rigor of the publication onto the product line.

2. **Single-Idea Cognitive Clarity**:
   - Rather than cluttering the screen with dense blocks of review text, an isolated quote slider focuses 100% of the reader's attention on a single powerful assertion at a time. This boosts message retention and emotional resonance.

3. **Restrained Editorial Typography**:
   - By rendering quotes in the store's primary serif typography (`var(--font-heading-family)`) with generous whitespace, the section feels like an authentic editorial pull-quote from a luxury print publication rather than an aggressive marketing badge.
