# Collection List Material Cards (Samsung Style)

Wide split-layout collection cards pairing editorial typography, individual pastel tints, and edge-touching photography in a horizontal carousel.

## Purpose

Feature hero categories or seasonal campaigns in high-impact wide cards that pair qualitative storytelling with direct collection action buttons.

## Physical composition

- **Header:** Clean title and circular `<` and `>` arrow navigation buttons.
- **Carousel Rail:** 2-column desktop / 1.05-column mobile scroll-snap rail of rounded pastel cards (`border-radius: 24px`).
- **Split Card Anatomy:** Left half contains category eyebrow, headline, description, and black pill CTA button; right half features full-height portrait or product photography.

## Psychology

Combines the clarity of curated collection categories with the narrative space of editorial features, lowering conversion friction via dedicated pill buttons.
