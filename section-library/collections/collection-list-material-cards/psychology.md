# Collection List Material Cards Psychology

Behavioral considerations and decision ergonomics of Samsung/Material style split collection cards.

## Cognitive ergonomics

1. **Dual Coding (Verbal + Pictorial Co-Presence):**
   - Placing descriptive copy and photography side-by-side inside a single colored boundary engages dual-coding theory—users process the lifestyle imagery and the specific category proposition simultaneously without eye jumping.
2. **Color Categorization (Soft Pastel Tints):**
   - Individual background card tints provide clear chromatic chunking between different categories, helping shoppers differentiate collections instantly.
3. **Explicit Call-to-Action Affordance:**
   - Dedicated pill buttons within each card clearly delineate the click zone and prime shoppers for a specific department transition (e.g. "Shop Accessories" vs "Shop Skincare").
