# Collection List Bento Psychology

## Cognitive Impact & Design Mechanics

1. **Combating Banner Blindness with Asymmetry**:
   - Uniform grids of identical rectangles frequently trigger habituation, where shoppers' eyes skim past categories without absorbing individual titles.
   - The alternating 1:2 and 2:1 bento grid introduces natural visual syncopation, forcing micro-fixations that increase engagement and category dwell time.

2. **Categorical Scaffolding**:
   - By presenting four distinct lifestyle categories in a unified bento cluster, the store establishes clear conceptual boundaries (e.g., tops vs. outer layers vs. essentials) without overwhelming the visitor with hundreds of individual product SKUs.

3. **Pill Badge Grounding**:
   - Positioning the pill badge in the lower-left corner leaves the visual center of the illustration or photography unobstructed.
   - High-contrast white backing creates an instant focal waypoint, allowing users to scan titles in under 200 milliseconds.
