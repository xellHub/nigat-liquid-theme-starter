# Featured Collection Carousel Psychology

Understanding cognitive flow and customer decision mechanics within the tabbed multi-collection carousel.

## Cognitive ergonomics

1. **Categorical Scoping (Tabs):**
   - By clustering inventory into recognizable lifestyle contexts (_Chairs_, _Living Room_, _Bedroom_, _Dining Room_), customers can jump straight to their intent without experiencing analysis paralysis.
2. **Horizontal Momentum (Carousel):**
   - Horizontal snap scrolling reduces vertical scroll fatigue on homepages while signaling continuous catalog depth.
3. **Legibility & Reassurance (Slide Index):**
   - The numerical index (`01 / 06` with active indicator) gives visitors an explicit mental model of where they are and how much content remains, eliminating the ambiguity common in unnumbered carousels.
4. **Visual Serenity:**
   - Soft studio frames (`#f6f6f6`) and understated editorial typography elevate perceived product craft and premium brand stature.
