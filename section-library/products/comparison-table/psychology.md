# Psychology & Decision Support — Comparison Table

## Cognitive Architecture

1. **Choice Paradox & Decision Paralysis Relief**:
   - Skincare and technical product catalogs often induce analysis paralysis. When shoppers cannot immediately distinguish which formula matches their skin type or use-case, conversion drops.
   - Structured comparison matrices present binary or graded compatibility across parallel vectors, accelerating cognitive triage.
2. **Asymmetric Anchoring (Column 1 Tinting)**:
   - Tinting the primary column in warm butter/cream (`#FFF9EE`) immediately registers as the visual anchor.
   - Consumers intuitively evaluate other options relative to the anchor, reinforcing its versatility and comprehensive coverage.
3. **Preattentive Checkmark Scanning**:
   - High-contrast green checkmarks (`✓`) provide instant visual confirmation without requiring dense paragraph comprehension.
4. **Modular Conclusion & Takeaway Reinforcement**:
   - Composable heading, muted subtext, and call-to-action blocks beneath the matrix crystallize the value proposition and guide the shopper directly to checkout.
