# Psychology & Decision Support — Events List

## Cognitive Architecture

1. **Spatial Separation of Logistics vs Experience**:
   - Customers considering live experiences first scan for _practical feasibility_ ("Can I make that time?") before evaluating emotional value.
   - Dedicating the left column to high-contrast dates and times eliminates logistical friction instantly.
2. **Micro-Pills for Reduced Cognitive Load**:
   - Pricing (`$110`), format (`VIRTUAL EVENT` / `IN-PERSON`), and category (`MASTERCLASS`) are presented as lightweight pre-attentive chips. This allows shoppers to filter eligibility in milliseconds.
3. **Sensory Appetite Appeal**:
   - The right column provides dedicated real estate for high-contrast imagery, activating visceral desire and aspirational association before ticket commitment.
4. **Transparent Scheduling Over Fake Scarcity**:
   - Clean calendar scheduling builds authentic brand prestige and trustworthiness without resorting to countdown countdowns or coercive urgency timers.
