# Marquee Showcase Psychology

Understanding visual momentum, social inclusion, and community conversion within the infinite marquee showcase.

## Cognitive ergonomics

1. **Continuous Motion & Visual Magnetism:**
   - Subtle, unhurried horizontal movement naturally catches peripheral vision at the bottom of long pages, refreshing customer attention before they reach the footer.
2. **Belonging & In-Group Appeal ("Join the family"):**
   - Framing community membership ("family") rather than commercial transactional commands ("Follow our account") activates social identity theory and in-group desire.
3. **Control Affordances (Pause on Hover):**
   - Automatic pausing upon hover gives users complete visual autonomy to inspect intriguing looks, preventing sensory overload or frustration from fleeing content.
