# Interactive Steps Psychology

Understanding customer cognitive load, routine building, and conversion momentum within the interactive step showcase.

## Cognitive ergonomics

1. **Procedural Chunking:**
   - Instead of presenting a complex catalog or intimidating regimen all at once, chunking into numbered chronological steps (`STEP 1`, `STEP 2`, `STEP 3`) drastically reduces cognitive friction and positions the merchant as a trusted guide.
2. **Visual Continuity & Sensory Engagement:**
   - Pairing the active step with synchronized media on the opposite column creates immediate visual cause-and-effect. Customers feel in control of their exploration.
3. **Pill Capsule Micro-Affordances:**
   - The contrast between monochrome inactive pills and the vibrant, full-color active pill signals state intuitively without noisy badges or redundant labels.
4. **Focused Conversion Pathway:**
   - By revealing the specific `Shop [Category]` button only for the actively inspected step, attention remains singularly focused on evaluating and taking action on that specific product category.
