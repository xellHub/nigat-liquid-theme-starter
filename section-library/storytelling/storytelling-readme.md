# Storytelling

Narrative families combining text, imagery, video, sequencing, and editorial rhythm.

## Purpose

Organize related storytelling section families so variants can be compared by structure and behavior before installation.

## Physical composition

Storytelling families arrange media and prose into an intentional sequence using splits, grids, carousels, comparisons, or editorial scale shifts.

## Psychology

They create context and emotional continuity before asking for action. Narrative should clarify genuine value rather than conceal essential commercial facts.

## Best used when

- the audience benefits from context, sequence, demonstration, or a reason to care before acting.
- The surrounding page gives the composition enough context and space to perform its role.

**Decision rule:** Best used when the audience benefits from context, sequence, demonstration, or a reason to care before acting; not when customers need a fast transactional answer or direct comparison.

## Avoid when

- customers need a fast transactional answer or direct comparison.
- A simpler section can communicate the same information with less interaction or visual weight.

## Good usage

Use this group to compare its families against the actual content and customer task, then descend into one family to select a concrete variant.

## Bad usage

Using the Storytelling group as a visual mood category leads to mismatched behavior. Choose it only when its customer task matches the page.

## Content requirements

- **Required:** A coherent narrative progression and media that contributes information.
- **Recommended:** One idea per section, concise transitions, and an optional action after the story resolves.
- **Unsupported:** Decorative media sequences with no informational relationship or inaccessible autoplay.

## Accessibility requirements

- Keep narrative order identical in visual and document flow.
- Caption meaningful video and describe informative imagery.
- Provide controls for motion and respect reduced-motion preferences.

## AI-agent guidance

- **Choose this when:** the requested composition matches this group’s purpose: the audience benefits from context, sequence, demonstration, or a reason to care before acting
- **Reject this when:** customers need a fast transactional answer or direct comparison
- **Prefer instead:** a product, collections, or text family.
- **Required evidence:** The page objective, content inventory, interaction requirements, and accessibility constraints.
