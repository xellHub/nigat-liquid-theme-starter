# Image Compare Psychology

Understanding why direct visual comparison builds customer trust and reduces hesitation.

## Cognitive anchors

1. **Eliminating Ambiguity:**
   When evaluating nuances in cuts, colors, or materials, textual claims are often doubted. An interactive visual slider allows users to self-verify differences instantly.

2. **Micro-Interactions Build Investment:**
   Interacting with the slider provides tactile agency. Active participation increases attention retention and product appreciation.

3. **Focused Decisions:**
   By focusing on a single, clear comparison pair with two straightforward call-to-actions ("View all" and "Shop now"), cognitive fatigue is avoided.
