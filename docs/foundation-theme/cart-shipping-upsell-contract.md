# F11 shipping progress and cart upsell contract

The drawer accepts `cart-shipping-progress` and `_cart-upsell` as independently selectable blocks inside its supplemental panel region. Neither makes a storefront promise by default. A merchant must add and enable the progress block, set a positive whole-unit threshold, and enter the exact cart currency ISO code. A market handle may further restrict it. The merchant must configure a corresponding Shopify shipping rate; this theme block only displays progress and never changes checkout shipping charges.

The progress basis is the sum of shippable lines in the presentment currency after their `discount_allocations`. Each line starts at `original_line_price` and subtracts its allocations. Digital-only and empty carts, zero thresholds, unset or mismatched currencies, and mismatched markets show no progress. Shopify represents prices in subunits, including a hundredfold representation for zero-decimal currencies, so a threshold entered in currency units is multiplied by 100. Remaining amounts use Shopify's `money` filter; percentage is clamped to 0–100. The visual bar updates when the drawer section re-renders. The script announces only a below-to-reached transition to assistive technology. [Cart and currency objects](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/liquid/objects/cart), [line-item discount allocations](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/liquid/objects/line_item), [market handle](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/liquid/objects/market).

The upsell block takes an ordered merchant `product_list`. It renders the F07 repeated static `product-card` slot with F08 quick actions for each available product not already present in the cart. There is no automatic recommendation fallback. Intro headings and text are child atoms; the product-card slot's image, title and price are independently editable. The parent supplies only grid layout. Sold-out, blank and already-in-cart candidates are omitted before rendering. Section replacement after cart add/change updates the candidate set; market changes require Shopify to render the section in the new localization context. The card slot should keep its `closest.product` binding so its displayed product matches the selected candidate. [Resource context for static blocks](https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/themes/architecture/blocks/theme-blocks/dynamic-sources).
